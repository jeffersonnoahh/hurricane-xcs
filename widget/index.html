Dashboard widgets for your iPhoneWidget dashboard di layar iPhone

Today's revenue, target, this month, top sales and a 14-day chart — right on your home screen or lock screen. Read-only: it can't change anything.

Omzet hari ini, target, omzet bulan ini, top sales, dan grafik 14 hari — langsung di layar utama atau layar kunci. Hanya membaca data, tidak bisa mengubah apa pun.

  1. Install the free app Scriptable from the App Store. Open Scriptable in the App Store
  2. Tap the green button above to copy the script.
  3. Open Scriptable, tap + (top right), then press and hold → Paste. Tap the title "Untitled Script" at the top, rename it Hurricane, then tap Done.
  4. On your home screen: press and hold an empty spot → Edit → Add Widget → search Scriptable → pick a size (small, medium or large).
  5. Press and hold the new widget → Edit Widget → Script: choose Hurricane. Leave "When Interacting" as it is — tapping the widget opens the dashboard.
  6. Optional, lock screen: press and hold the lock screen → Customize → Lock Screen → add a Scriptable widget → choose Hurricane.
  1. Pasang aplikasi Scriptable (gratis) dari App Store. Buka Scriptable di App Store
  2. Ketuk tombol hijau di atas untuk menyalin script.
  3. Buka Scriptable, ketuk + di kanan atas, lalu tekan lama → Paste. Ketuk judul "Untitled Script" di atas, ganti jadi Hurricane, lalu Done.
  4. Di layar utama: tekan lama bagian kosong → Edit → Add Widget → cari Scriptable → pilih ukuran (kecil, sedang, atau besar).
  5. Tekan lama widget yang baru → Edit Widget → Script: pilih Hurricane. Biarkan "When Interacting" apa adanya — mengetuk widget membuka dashboard.
  6. Opsional, layar kunci: tekan lama layar kunci → Customize → Lock Screen → tambah widget Scriptable → pilih script Hurricane.

Calendar widget — Hurricane styleWidget Kalender — gaya Hurricane

Pitch black like the Hurricane widget: the day, the date and your next events in your Google Calendar colours. It reads the iPhone Calendar app (Google Calendar is included once your Google account is added in Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts). Read-only.

Hitam pekat seperti widget Hurricane: hari, tanggal, dan acara berikutnya dengan warna kalender Google-mu. Membaca app Kalender iPhone (Google Calendar ikut kalau akun Google sudah ditambahkan di Pengaturan → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts). Hanya membaca.

  1. In Scriptable: + → press and hold → Paste → rename it Calendar → Done.
  2. Tap the Calendar tile once. Your iPhone asks about calendar access → tap Allow Full Access. Without this step the widget only shows "allow calendar access".
  3. Add a Scriptable widget like steps 4–5 above, but choose the script Calendar. Tapping the widget opens the Google Calendar app.
  1. Di Scriptable: + → tekan lama → Paste → ganti judul jadi Kalender → Done.
  2. Ketuk tile Kalender sekali. iPhone bertanya soal kalender → pilih Allow Full Access (Izinkan akses penuh). Tanpa langkah ini widget hanya menampilkan pesan "izinkan akses kalender".
  3. Tambah widget Scriptable seperti langkah 4–5 di atas, tapi pilih script Kalender. Ketuk widget = buka app Google Calendar.

Already installed it in Indonesian? Copy again here, open your script in Scriptable, tap inside the code → Select All → Paste → Done. The widget on your home screen switches language by itself.

iOS decides when widgets refresh (usually every 15–60 minutes). The small clock in the corner is the last update; "offline" means no internet right now and the last numbers are shown. Tap the widget for the latest numbers.

Raw scripts: hurricane-widget.js · kalender-widget.js (Indonesian by default; change LANG to 'en').

iOS yang mengatur jadwal pembaruan widget (biasanya tiap ±15–60 menit). Jam kecil di pojok widget = terakhir diperbarui; tulisan "offline" berarti sedang tidak ada internet dan angka terakhir yang tampil. Ketuk widget untuk angka terbaru.
