Performer notes v37

Hold the time for two seconds to open this page. Nobody else should see it.

Secret keypad

When the stopwatch shows 00.00,00, the big time area above the buttons is an invisible keypad. Tap it briefly; a swipe changes the page and a two second hold opens these notes:

1  2  3
4  5  6
7  8  9
*  0  C

C clears. What you typed shows for two seconds in very dim text at the bottom left corner.

Fixing a wrong number

  • Tap C on the keypad, or hold the grey Lap button for one second. The corner shows a dash.
  • The number is used once. After the stop and Reset, the stopwatch is honest again.

Alarm already set

Type a time as four digits on the stopwatch keypad, for example 0742. The Alarms tab then shows an alarm at 07.42, switched on, in its right place in the list. It stays until you clear the number with C or by holding Lap. It must be a real time: hours up to 23, minutes up to 59.

Alarm by scrolling

On the Alarms tab, the place where you put your finger down to start a scroll is the input. Each alarm card has a left half and a right half.

  • Cards 1 to 6 (the first two rows): the hour. Halves count 1, 2, 3 ... 12. One touch is the whole hour: 1 means 1 o'clock, 11 means 11 o'clock. You never enter a zero first.
  • Cards 7 to 12: the first minute digit. Halves count 1 to 5. The top part of card 7 is 0.
  • Cards 13 to 18: the last minute digit. Halves count 1 to 9. The top part of card 13 is 0.
  • After the third digit it locks. An alarm far down the list, about two scrolls below the input cards, becomes that time, switched on. The list stays in time order, so other alarms change around it. A time before about 3 in the morning cannot be that deep, because not enough times come before it. Cards only change while they are off screen, so scroll back to the top before you hand it over.
  • C, clear and start again: card 18, the last card of the third group, is the C key. Tap it, or start a scroll on it. It works at any time, also after the time has locked. The CHANGE button and the Alarms title clear too.
  • Afternoon (adds 12 hours): tap the big 11.00 on the first card, or Edit.
  • The corner shows what you have entered, like 7:4_ and then 07.42.

Both tricks from one input

Either input loads both tabs. A time entered by scrolling on Alarms also loads the stopwatch: a run of Start, Lap, Stop then gives a lap ending in the hour and a lap ending in the minutes. A four digit time typed on the stopwatch keypad also appears in Alarms.

What it does

  • Two digits (73): whenever he stops, the hundredths are 73.
  • Four or six digits (1403, 140391): each press of Lap, and the final Stop, shows the next pair. Lap 1 ends in 14, lap 2 in 03, lap 3 in 91. A long number waits for a run that uses Lap, so plain start and stop runs before it stay honest and he can test freely. With the phone open, the pairs appear in the TOTAL column, and the last pair is also in the big digits when he stops. Folded or sideways, they appear in the lap time.
  • Four digits then * (1973*): exact mode. Stop always shows 00.19,73, however long it ran. Use it with the screen face down. The first two digits must be 59 or less.

Notes

  • The number is used once. After the stop and Reset it is cleared.
  • With no number loaded it is an honest stopwatch.
